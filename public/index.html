    ABOUT

    Built for a simple flow.

    Jaduloader is a static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript frontend connected to a separately configured API backend. It has no account system and no third-party frontend scripts.

    FAQ

    Quick answers

    Why might a YouTube download fail?

    YouTube may block automated access or a video may be private, unavailable, or restricted. The backend does not use sign-in cookies or bypass those restrictions.

    Where is my history stored?

    Recent-download history is saved locally in this browser and is not sent to the backend.
